2

What the service does, and what it does not do

EasyESG asks you the disclosures of the VSME voluntary standard as ordinary questions, calculates what can be calculated from your answers, and produces a report you can send to a bank, a buyer or a tender committee. The service is software. It does not include advice.

We are not an auditor, a certification body, a consultant or your accountant. We do not submit your report to any authority, register or platform. Whether a report satisfies a particular request is decided by whoever asked for it, not by us. The figures, the explanations and the decision to publish are yours.

3

Your account and the people you invite

An account belongs to one person and is not shared. Keep the password and the second factor to yourself; anything done with your credentials is treated as done by you. Tell us at once if you think someone else has access.

Whoever creates the organisation becomes its administrator and decides who else may read, fill in, or approve a report. You are responsible for those choices, including for external people such as your accountant or an adviser. Removing a person removes their access immediately; the record of what they entered stays in the report history.

4

Your report content stays yours

You keep every right in the answers, documents and figures you put into EasyESG. You give us permission to store, process and display that content for the single purpose of running the service for you — nothing else. We do not sell it, do not use it for advertising, and do not use it to train models.

We publish counts that cannot identify anyone — how many reports were completed in a month, which sections take longest. If we ever want to describe your company by name, we ask first. The privacy notice and the data processing agreement say in detail how content is handled; where they conflict with this clause, they win.

5

Plans, payment and invoices

Filling in a report is free. Payment is due when you generate a finished document, and the price of your plan is shown before you confirm. Prices are in Moldovan lei and include VAT where it applies. You can pay by card or by bank transfer; bank transfers need the payment reference we give you, or the payment cannot be matched to your account. An invoice follows each payment, by e-Factura if you ask for it.

5.1
A subscription renews on its own date each period until you cancel it. Saving a card for renewals is a separate authorisation you give explicitly, not a consequence of paying once.
5.2
If we change a price, the new price applies from your next renewal and we tell you at least 30 days before it. You can cancel before then and keep the old price until the period ends.
5.3
Generated documents are not refundable once produced. If the service was unavailable for a substantial part of a paid period, write to us and we will credit that period.

Where liability can be limited by law, ours is limited to the fees you paid in the twelve months before the claim. Nothing here limits liability for intentional harm or for anything the law of the Republic of Moldova does not allow to be limited.

Data is encrypted in transit and at rest. 3

What we do not set

No advertising cookies. No cross-site tracking. No social network buttons or embedded players. No third-party script of any kind on a screen where you are signed in — which is why there is no long list of vendors on this page, and why refusing statistics costs you nothing.

Our public pages load two fonts from a font service and nothing else. If you would rather they did not, the site is fully readable with the fonts blocked.

4

Storage that is not a cookie

While you fill in a report, your browser keeps a local copy of the answers so a lost connection does not lose your work. It sits in local storage under esg_draft_cache, never leaves your device on its own, and is cleared when you sign out or when the report is submitted.

This is part of the service working as intended, so it is not something you can switch off separately. Signing out removes it.

5

Changing your mind, or your browser

The switch above is the whole mechanism, and “Cookie choices” sits in the footer of every page. Turning statistics off deletes the esg_stats cookie immediately; nothing collected before that is linked to you in any way that could be undone.

You can also clear cookies in your browser. If you clear the necessary ones you will be signed out, the site will forget your language, and it will ask about statistics again on the next visit.
