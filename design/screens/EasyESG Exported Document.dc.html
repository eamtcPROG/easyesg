How to read the marks

Four marks appear in the tables and headings of this report. Each is drawn in outline and weight, never in colour, and each carries its own words. A photocopy of this page loses nothing.

Not materialThe undertaking considered the disclosure and judged it not material. The rationale is printed in full where the mark appears.
Not availableThe disclosure applies, and the undertaking could not obtain the figure for this period. The stated reason is printed with it.
Nil returnThe answer is nothing, deliberately: no incidents, no fines, no convictions. This is a zero, not a gap.
UnresolvedA check raised a question the undertaking did not settle before filing. Both values are printed so the reader can judge.

The body

Every module page carries the same furniture: a running header naming the undertaking and the section, a rule, the section number and its official label, and a footer with the standard version and the page counter. Indicator tables always show the comparative period beside the current one, the unit sits in its own column so no break can separate a figure from its unit, and a substituted figure carries its attribution on the same page as the number, not in an appendix.

B1
Basis for preparation

This report has been prepared in accordance with the Basic Module of the voluntary sustainability reporting standard for non-listed small and medium-sized undertakings (VSME), taxonomy version 2025.1. It covers the financial year from 1 January to 31 December 2025.

The report is prepared on an individual basis. Fornaio Bellini SRL is a subsidiary of Grupul Lina SRL; no consolidated sustainability report is prepared at group level for this period, and no figures of other group undertakings are included here.

The undertaking operates a bakery in Chișinău and a distribution depot in Ialoveni. Both sites are within the reporting boundary. Its principal activity is the manufacture of bread, fresh pastry goods and cakes (NACE 10.71).

Reasoned omissions and unresolved findings

A gap is an answer, and it is printed as one. This page sits before the body rather than after it: a reader who has to hunt for the omissions concludes they were hidden. Each entry names the disclosure, the mark, the reason in the undertaking's own words, and the page where the mark appears again. The unresolved inconsistency prints both figures — the report does not choose between them on the undertaking's behalf.

Fornaio Bellini SRL · Sustainability report 2025 Omissions and findings
Reasoned omissions and unresolved findings

Three disclosures of the Basic Module are not answered with a figure, and one check remains unresolved. Nothing else in this report is blank.

B5 Biodiversity Not material p. 9

Stated by the undertaking: “Both our sites are inside built-up areas of Chișinău and Ialoveni. Neither is in or adjacent to a protected area or a key biodiversity area, and we hold no land outside the two premises. We judge biodiversity not material to our activity for this period.”

Recorded 22 February 2026 by Ana Rusu, owner-administrator. Reversible in a later period if the activity changes.

B6 Water withdrawal, total for the period Not available p. 9

Stated by the undertaking: “The Ialoveni depot is billed through the landlord on a shared meter. We asked for a 2025 breakdown in January 2026 and have not received one. The Chișinău figure alone would understate withdrawal, so we report neither.”

A separate meter was installed in February 2026; the figure is expected to be available for the 2026 report.

B10 Gender pay gap Not applicable p. 14

The disclosure applies to undertakings with 150 employees or more. Fornaio Bellini SRL employed 34 people at the end of the period, so the standard does not require it. This is a condition of the standard, not a choice of the undertaking.

B7 Total waste generated, against the sum of streams Unresolved p. 10

The total reported for the period is 41,8 t. The four streams reported separately sum to 39,2 t, a difference of 2,6 t. The undertaking filed without reconciling the two figures. Both are printed here and in table B7.1.

12.5 · the other output
The Excel file has no page design here, deliberately

It is the official EFRAG workbook. The platform contributes values into the template's own cells and changes nothing about its presentation — no added sheets, no reformatting, no branding. Its only design surface is the export dialogue that produces it, which lives in EasyESG Reporting Core.

What the print layer shares with the screen

Colour roles, the state vocabulary, the space scale and every string. What it does not share: the type family, the measure, the grid, and the density. A document read once by a stranger is not laid out like a form filled in over six weeks.
