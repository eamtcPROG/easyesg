Behaviour the drawing has to be read with
It is never a step, and never modal

The panel is present from the first field to the last and can be collapsed to a count in the contextual bar. Nothing in the wizard waits on it, and no navigation is refused because of it (NFR-76).

One sentence per problem, the same one twice

The panel row and the field message are the same text. A person who reads it in the panel and then walks to the field is not asked to reconcile two descriptions of one problem.

Severity separates blocking from advisory

Must fix means the report would be wrong or incomplete as filed. Review means it looks unusual and a human should decide. Reasoned gaps are neither — they are listed so they can be re-read, not corrected.

Counts move as you type, the list settles when you leave the field

Rows do not disappear mid-keystroke. A resolved item moves to “Cleared in this session” with its module named, so progress is legible without the list rearranging under the cursor.

Read-only and offline states apply here too

On a filed or locked report the panel still lists and still links, with every fix affordance removed. Offline, the header reads “Checked before you went offline” and the count is stated as of that moment rather than implied to be live.

The panel reuses the eight-state vocabulary from §7.3 rather than inventing a second severity language: iris for reasoned, amber for review, red for blocking, and no marker for coherent answers. Grouping is by module, ordered as the rail orders them, so the panel and the rail can be read against each other.

Behaviour the drawing has to be read with
Reached from the field, never only from an audit table

Every field that has changed carries a count and a link in its own footer (UX-68). The same panel serves the administrator surfaces, where it opens over the entity or period record instead of the wizard.

Attribution outlives access

A person removed from the organization keeps their entries, marked as removed rather than anonymised (UX-69). The removal dialogue states this before it is confirmed, so nobody expects the trail to be cleared with the account.

Every entry names its source

Typed, carried forward from last year, calculated by the carbon calculator, or overridden — the source is stated in the same line as the time, because a value's provenance is what a reviewer is actually asking about.

Restoring is a change, not an undo

Restoring an earlier value appends an entry attributed to whoever restored it. Nothing in the list is ever removed or rewritten, and on a filed or locked report the restore affordances are absent while the list still reads.

What changes, and what does not

At 834 the six module states survive as coloured dots on chips; at 390 they move behind “All modules”, which opens the full rail as a list. The current module is always named on screen.

The count is the panel's permanent seat

Save stays automatic, the indicator gets shorter

“Saved 12:04” replaces the full four-state indicator in the header; the queued and failed states still take over the whole bar when they occur, because they are the only ones a person must act on.

Fields keep every affordance

Not material, not applicable, not available and the field history stay on the field at 390 rather than being folded into an overflow menu. Inputs go to 48px, and the conditional-reason chip wraps above the input instead of sitting beside the label.
