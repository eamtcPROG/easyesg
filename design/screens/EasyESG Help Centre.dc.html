Answered without a sales call
Does my company have to report at all?

Most small companies in Moldova are not obliged by law. They are asked by a bank, a buyer or a parent company — and that request usually has a deadline attached. This article explains who asks, and what they accept.

How long does a first report take?

Between three and six hours of work spread over a few weeks, most of it spent finding invoices. You can stop after any section; nothing is lost between sessions.

Who can see the figures I enter?

Only the people you invite, until you export or share a report yourself. We do not file anything on your behalf and we do not pass data to any authority.

What does it cost, and can I see the questions first?

All eleven basic sections are listed in full on the public site, before any payment. Plans and their limits are on the pricing page; a first report can be completed on the free tier.

B3 · Energy and emissions

We rent our premises and the landlord pays the electricity. What do I enter?

3 min read·Updated 4 August 2026·Reviewed against VSME by the content team
The short answer

Enter the electricity your own operations used, even when the invoice is in the landlord's name. If the lease charges you a share of the building's bill, that share is your figure. If you genuinely cannot obtain it, say so in the field's note — an explained gap is accepted, an empty field is not.

Why the standard asks this

B3 asks about energy your business consumes, not energy your business is invoiced for. Whoever reads your report — a bank, a buyer — is trying to understand the footprint of the activity, and a rented bakery consumes electricity whether or not the meter is in its name.

This is also why the wizard asks for premises and floor area in B1 before it asks about energy: a share of a shared bill is only defensible if the basis of the share is recorded.

Worked example: a leased bakery

Building bill, 2026kWh
Whole building, as invoiced to the landlord184 200
Your floor area share, per the lease (312 m² of 1 040 m²)30 %
Sub-metered oven circuit, billed to you directly21 400
Entered in B3 · purchased electricity76 660

55 260 kWh from the floor-area share plus the 21 400 kWh you were billed directly. Use the sub-meter wherever one exists; fall back on a share only for what is not measured.

If the landlord will not tell you

Ask once, in writing, for the annual building consumption and your share basis. If it does not arrive, mark the field not available and write what you asked for and when. The report stays valid and the reader sees an honest boundary instead of a zero that would be wrong.

Do not enter zero. Zero means the premises used no electricity, which the validation will contradict against your headcount and floor area, and the finding will hold the report at draft.

What to write in the note

Two sentences are enough. Name the source, the basis and the limit:

“Electricity is invoiced to the landlord for the whole building. Our figure is 30 % of the 2026 building consumption, the floor-area share stated in the lease, plus the sub-metered oven circuit billed to us directly.”
