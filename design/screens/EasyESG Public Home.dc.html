Your bank asked for it

Or a large customer, or a tender. The questionnaire landed on your desk, and there is nobody in the company whose job this is.

The standard is unreadable

VSME was written for accountants. We restate every question in plain language and keep the official wording one click away for the people who want it.

You won't have every number

Nobody does in the first year. Say what you don't have and why, in a sentence your reader will see. That is a disclosure, not a failure.

How it works

Four steps. Stop after any of them.

Your answers save as you type, so you can build the report in twenty-minute sessions over a month. Nothing leaves the platform until you export it yourself.

1
Tell us about the company

Name, IDNO and the year you are reporting on. The year fixes which version of the standard applies, so it cannot change under you.

2
Answer the sections

Your first answers decide what you are asked afterwards. A ten-person bakery is never shown the questions written for a factory.

3
Check your work

Press check whenever you like. Each finding tells you the field, the rule and what it would take to settle it — then takes you there.

4
Export and send it

A PDF for reading and an Excel file for anyone who wants the figures. You send them. We do not file anything on your behalf.

The questions

Eleven sections. This is all of them.

The VSME Basic Module, in the order you will meet it. Most companies answer seven or eight and declare the rest immaterial with a reason.

Open the full question list
B1
Basis for preparation
Who you are, how many people you employ, whether you report for one company or a group.
B2
Practices, policies and future initiatives
What you already do about sustainability, written in your own words.
B3
Energy and greenhouse gas emissions
Electricity, fuel and the emissions that follow. The calculator does the conversion.
B4
Pollution of air, water and soil
Only if you hold a permit that requires you to measure it.
B5
Biodiversity
Land you use, and whether any of it sits near a protected area.
B6
Water
What you withdraw and consume. Immaterial for most office-based companies.
B7
Waste and circular use of materials
Tonnes out, and how much of it is recovered.
B8
Workforce — general characteristics
Headcount, contract types, how many people joined and left.
B9
Workforce — health and safety
Accidents and days lost. Zero is an answer here, and we label it as one.
B10
Workforce — pay, bargaining and training
Wage floor, collective agreements, training hours per person.
B11
Convictions and fines for corruption
Usually a single affirmative zero, and it takes one click.
⊘
A section that does not apply to you is declared immaterial with a rationale, and stops counting against your progress.

Questions people ask first

Before you sign up

If something here is still unclear, the help centre has the long version, in all three languages.

Open the help centre
Do I need an accountant or a consultant?

No. If you can find last year's electricity bills and your payroll headcount, you can finish a report. Your accountant can be invited to check the figures if you want a second pair of eyes.

How long does it take?

A first report is usually a few sessions rather than one sitting, because you will go looking for two or three numbers. Everything saves as you type, so stopping costs you nothing.

What if I don't have a number?

Declare it unavailable and write one sentence saying why. The sentence appears in the report. Readers accept a stated reason; they do not accept a blank.

Is the report filed with an authority?

No. VSME is voluntary. You export the report and send it to whoever asked — a bank, a customer, a tender committee. We never submit anything on your behalf.

What happens next year?

Your 2025 answers sit beside the 2026 fields, and anything unchanged carries over in one click. The second report takes a fraction of the time.

Can someone else answer parts of it?

Yes. Invite a colleague or your bookkeeper with view or edit access. Every answer stays attributed to the person who entered it, so you always know who wrote what.

Your data

Where your answers sit, and who can see them

Hosted in the European Union

Your report and its history stay on EU infrastructure, encrypted in transit and at rest.

Nothing is published

Your figures are visible to the people you invite and to nobody else. There is no public directory of reports.

Support cannot look in uninvited

If you ask for help with a specific field, you grant time-limited access, and the visit is written to your audit log.

You can take it with you

Export the full report and its underlying figures at any time, in PDF and Excel, for as long as your account exists.

Open your 2025 report today

An email address is enough to start. You will see every question, and every section, before anyone asks you to pay.
