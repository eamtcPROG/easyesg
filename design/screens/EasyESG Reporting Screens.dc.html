EasyESG · Reporting · Desktop screens at 1440

The reporting core as screens

Thirty-three desktop frames covering the whole reporting core: every step of the Basic Module from B1 to B11 with its full field set, then review, export, a filed report, a revision comparison, creating a report, marking one as filed, collaboration in the wizard, evidence and field history, the Comprehensive module added mid-report, an offline session, a report locked by a colleague, the export states, and a second year opening on carried-forward values. Nine more frames follow for the Comprehensive module, C1 to C9, once the bank has asked for it: the same wizard with a second spine in the sidebar, derived figures drawn from Basic answers, and gaps recorded with a reason rather than left blank. Every part is drawn from the anatomy in EasyESG Reporting Core, which stays as the specification.
