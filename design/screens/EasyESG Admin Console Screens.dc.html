EasyESG · Administrative console · Desktop screens at 1440

The console the platform is run from

Twenty-two desktop frames covering the administrative surfaces A-01 to A-18: platform administration — the organization register, content and translation, taxonomy versions and migration runs, factor sets, validation rules, adoption, support access, admin accounts and identity providers — and the back office — plans and pricing, reconciliation, collections, invoicing, e-Factura exceptions, refunds, enterprise contracts and the billing ledger.

Same tokens as the tenant application, deliberately different composition: compact density, exception queues rather than wizards, keyboard-first, and no responsive pass below wide — the console states that rather than degrading. Every cross-tenant operation is drawn in one shape: preview, then how many organizations and reports it touches, then confirm, then progress, then result with a way back. Every manual financial resolution is drawn with the rationale field it requires.
